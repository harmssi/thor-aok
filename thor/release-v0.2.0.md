## Thor AoK 0.2.0: play with the sticks

### New
- **Sticks and buttons:** the left stick moves a pointer, A selects, B orders, the right stick scrolls the map (L2 held: zoom). X = next idle villager, L1 = Town Center, R1 = next idle military unit, Y = back, Start = menu.
- **Control groups on the D-pad:** hold R2 and press a direction to set group 1 to 4, press the direction alone to pick it.
- **Newer engine:** OpenAoK up to 7 Nov 2026 (hawks, carcasses, Wonders and Gaia buildings drawn, Town Center ground walkable, Black Forest trees).

### Important when updating
This version needs **newer game data**. Run `python3 android/package.py --hd <Age2HD>` from this version again, copy `OpenAoK-data` to the Thor and choose the folder when the app asks. Saved games of 0.1.0 may not load.

### Tested on the Thor
Death Match from Dark Age to Feudal Age through the deck, barracks, houses, mill, seven militia as a control group marched across the map; 60 fps.

### Known limitations
- The Age of Kings civilisations and rules only (Conquerors content is not playable yet).
- No keyboard hotkeys, no campaigns. Converting the HD files still needs a PC or Mac.
